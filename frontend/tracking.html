Protocol Intelligence (FAQ)

What is a Manifest ID? expand_more

Your Manifest ID is a unique 12-digit alphanumeric code generated at the moment of booking. It serves as your neural key to access real-time telemetry data.

Can I reroute a shipment in transit? expand_more

Yes. However, rerouting requires a Level 2 authorization. Please visit the Command Center Hub to submit a vector-change request.

How accurate is the "Live View" location? expand_more

Our GPS telemetry is accurate within 3 meters. Note that orbital updates occur every 60 seconds for Aero-Priority shipments.
